Math Practice

Seven short practice pages. Each one keeps its own record on this device, under its Report tab.

  • Exact Trig ValuesFlash cards and a timed table for sin, cos and tan at the six key angles.
  • Unit Circle TrainerFind angles on the circle, read coordinates, and fill a blank circle from memory.
  • Fraction RepsShort sets on fraction and algebra steps, with a mixed check.
  • Six Trig GraphsName each of the six trig graphs, plot key points, and learn the facts for each.
  • Identity BuilderRecall the key identities and rebuild each one from the ones before it.
  • Half-Angle StepsWork half-angle problems one step at a time, then sprint.
  • Friday CheckFor a parent: quiz cards with answers, and the end-of-week checks.

Practice is saved in this browser only. Use the same device and browser each day so the record stays in one place.
