Sine counts up under the root

For the first five angles, write √0, √1, √2, √3, √4, each over 2. Three of them simplify. That is the sine column.

Cosine is the same list backwards. Tangent is sine divided by cosine. So there is one list to remember, not a table of separate facts.

The last row, π, sits outside the pattern. On the unit circle π is the far left point, (−1, 0). Cosine is the x value and sine is the y value, so cos π = −1 and sin π = 0.

Where the numbers come from

Two triangles give every value. Sine is the side opposite the angle divided by the longest side. Cosine is the side next to the angle divided by the longest side.

Across from the π/6 angle the side is 1, and the longest side is 2. So sin π/6 = 1/2 and cos π/6 = √3/2. Read it from the π/3 corner and the two swap.

Both short sides are 1 and the longest side is √2. So sin π/4 and cos π/4 are both 1/√2, which is written √2/2.

How to use this page

Do the cards for five minutes a day. Pulling an answer out of memory makes it stick better than reading the table again. When all eighteen show as automatic, take the table test and try to beat your best time.
